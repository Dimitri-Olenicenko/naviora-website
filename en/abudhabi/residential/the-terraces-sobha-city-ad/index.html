Overview

The Terraces at Sobha City is a thoughtfully master-planned residential development defined by contemporary architecture, open spatial planning, and a strong connection to nature. The project features a collection of garden villas designed with an emphasis on natural light, privacy, and seamless indoor-outdoor living. Residences are positioned to maximize views of landscaped greenery and water features, while private gardens and elevated terraces extend the living experience beyond interior spaces.

About Al Bahya

Al Bahia is an emerging coastal district in the northern part of Abu Dhabi, offering a peaceful residential atmosphere away from the city’s central bustle. Positioned along the highway connecting Abu Dhabi to Dubai, the area benefits from strategic accessibility while maintaining a calm, low-density environment. Its proximity to the coastline adds a natural charm, making it an attractive choice for those seeking a relaxed lifestyle with open surroundings.
