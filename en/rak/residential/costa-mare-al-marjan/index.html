Overview

Costa Mare is a striking waterfront development on Al Marjan Island, composed of four architecturally distinctive towers that harmonize with their natural surroundings. The design draws inspiration from both the sea and the nearby mountains, expressed through fluid forms, soft earthy tones, and expansive glazing that captures panoramic views. Each residence is thoughtfully planned to maximize light, space, and tranquility, creating a refined coastal environment that balances elegance with a sense of calm exclusivity.

About Al Marjan Island

Al Marjan Island is an exciting resort area located in the Emirate of Ras Al Khaimah. This island is the perfect place to relax and unwind on the shores of the Persian Gulf. Al Marjan is famous for its white-sand beaches, crystal clear waters and luxurious hotel complexes.

Al Marjan Island is a stunning man-made archipelago located in the emirate of Ras Al Khaimah. It is one of the most prominent tourist destinations in the region, known for its picturesque beaches, luxurious resorts, and upscale residential developments. Al Marjan Island is situated along the Arabian Gulf coastline and offers breathtaking views of the sea. The island is shaped like a coral reef and is made up of four primary coral-shaped islands, connected by a central boulevard.
