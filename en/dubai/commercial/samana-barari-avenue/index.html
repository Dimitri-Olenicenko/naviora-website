Overview

Samana Barari Avenue is a premium commercial development by Samana Developers, conceived as a business park reimagined for success in the heart of Majan. Blending cutting-edge architecture with high-end office spaces and prime retail outlets, it is a landmark destination where visionaries, entrepreneurs and established enterprises come together to reshape the future of business. Strategically positioned along Sheikh Mohammed bin Zayed Road, the project sits within easy reach of Dunecrest American School, Global Village, IMG Worlds of Adventure, Dubai Miracle Garden, Dubai Mall and Dubai International Airport.

About Majan

Majan is a modern residential community located in the Dubailand region, which is one of the largest and most diverse urban developments in Dubai. The Majan neighbourhood is ideal for family living due to its diverse infrastructure, comfortable living environment and convenient location. The neighbourhood offers a variety of property options, including apartments and villas, combining modern design and high quality construction.

Majan is a 1.5 million square meter district in Dubai. It is located at the heart of the large Dubailand community. Majan is a perfect place for those who are looking for a buy-to-invest property. While the community is developing, apartment prices have not yet reached their maximum values. If you want to purchase an off-plan housing unit, you can count on a good discount. With Sheikh Mohammed Bin Zayed Road at its front, Majan lies in close proximity to Al Ain Road.
