About project

Davit Bek 290 Residential Complex is located in the highest residential area of Yerevan, in Nor Nork's 5th Massif. The complex stands out for its prime location, offering high seismic resistance, clean air, and an environmentally friendly living environment. Advantages & Location Stunning views of Mount Ararat, St. Sarkis Church, and the Yerevan city skyline. Located at the intersection of Mikoyan and Davit Bek Streets, the complex provides fast and convenient access to the city's key infrastructure. The surrounding area offers all the essential amenities and infrastructure needed for a comfortable and fulfilling everyday lifestyle.
