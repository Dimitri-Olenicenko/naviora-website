A City Planned with Precision

A self-sustaining township designed as a complete urban destination where nature, wellbeing, and everyday living are seamlessly connected.

Planned around walkability and choice, the community brings together marina living, beach-inspired experiences, and forest-led neighbourhoods within one integrated city. Living options range from 1 and 2-bedroom residences to expansive 4 to 6 bedroom villas, thoughtfully positioned to offer privacy, openness, and connection to the surrounding landscape.

With intuitively placed amenities, expansive green corridors, and architecture shaped by context and climate, the city offers a balanced way of living rooted in comfort, movement, and a sense of belonging.

Homes Designed For Generations

Homes are thoughtfully distributed across distinct neighborhoods from beachfront apartments to villa communities, allowing residents to select a lifestyle that reflects how they want to live today and how they may evolve tomorrow.

From 1 and 2-bedroom residences to expansive 4 to 6 bedroom villas, the city offers homes that evolve with families over time — supporting different stages of life while keeping communities connected.

Connect

Where Community Takes Form

At Sobha City, connection is thoughtfully designed into everyday life. Tree-lined promenades, social courtyards, and waterfront walkways create natural moments of interaction where neighbors meet, families gather, and communities grow.  

Retail, cafés, and shared spaces are seamlessly woven into the urban fabric, extending life beyond the home and into a vibrant, walkable environment. Every space encourages connection, while still preserving a sense of privacy and calm.

Restore

Balance, Built into Daily Life

Nature at Sobha City is not an addition, it is infrastructure. Green corridors, shaded pathways, and water features are designed to restore both body and mind, creating a daily rhythm grounded in calm and clarity.

From meditation zones and wellness landscapes to quiet retreats under tree canopies, every element is intentionally placed to bring balance into everyday living. Here, stillness is not separate from life, it is part of it.
