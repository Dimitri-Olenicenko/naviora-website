About project

An environment is being created here, where importance will be given to the comfort of future residents and an increase in the quality of life. The main idea of ​​the project is that even a resident who chooses a standard-class apartment should feel a new level of life, lead a pleasant and healthy lifestyle thanks to the new environment. The residential complex will consist of five buildings. Each building will have 23 ground floors. 4 of the 5 buildings are designed for 3 floors of commercial premises, where shops, supermarkets and other services will operate. And the 5th building is designed for a 4-story business center
